OUR BEGINNING

A Little Bakery
With a Big Dream

Lahari's Crumb & Co. began its journey in 2007 with a simple dream — to create fresh, delicious baked goods that bring people together.

What started with a passion for baking grew into a bakery loved for its cakes, pastries, breads and everyday treats.

Through the years, we have stayed true to our roots while continuing to explore new flavours, designs and baking techniques.
